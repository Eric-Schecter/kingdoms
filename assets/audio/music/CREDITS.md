# Battlefield music

- Valley: original project-generated `storybook-battle.wav`, unchanged.
- Snow: **Through the Snow / Snow Theme**, Cleyton Kauffman (https://soundcloud.com/cleytonkauffman). CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/snow-theme . Original `BGM_Snow_Theme_Loop.ogg` extracted from snow_theme_1.zip; unmodified, 24-second seamless loop supplied by author.
- Graveyard: **Dark Theme**, JaggedStone. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/dark-theme . Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/theme_0.ogg . Original OGG used as repeating background music.
- Volcano: **Cynic Battle Loop**, Ferk's loop edit of **Battle Theme A** by Alex Smith / cynicmusic. CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/cynic-battle-loop . Composer: https://cynicmusic.com / https://pixelsphere.org . Original cynicbattleloop_0.ogg used unmodified.

CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Runtime applies per-track volume matching and a short fade-in on switching, preserving the music-on/off preference. No tracks are claimed as our compositions except the original valley loop.
